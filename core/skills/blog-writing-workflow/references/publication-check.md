# Publication check

Return one status:

- `READY`: every required check passed and unavailable checks are explicitly
  accepted by the user or non-blocking under the profile.
- `BLOCKED`: a required check failed, publication authority is missing, or the
  destination cannot be verified.

## Content

- The title states the actual subject rather than a vague mood.
- The opening establishes why the subject matters without repeating the title.
- The argument progresses without duplicated sections or unexplained jumps.
- Technical claims, numbers, quotes, and current facts are supported or marked
  as personal observation.
- The conclusion follows from the body and does not introduce a new thesis.
- Spelling, spacing, code fences, headings, and links were checked.

## Voice and metadata

- The draft follows author-confirmed profile rules rather than imitating a
  single sample post.
- Required frontmatter fields, filename, date, excerpt, tags, and image metadata
  match the site contract.
- The excerpt explains the post's value and contains no private disclaimer or
  accidental internal identifier.
- Images have meaningful alternative text where the platform supports it.

## Privacy and safety

- Names, internal URLs, repository identifiers, ticket keys, credentials,
  customer data, and copied conversations are absent unless deliberately public.
- Examples preserve the lesson while removing unnecessary identifying detail.
- The draft does not reproduce substantial source text or claim another
  person's voice as the author's own.

## Delivery

- The target file or platform and exact publication action are known.
- The user explicitly authorized publication, not merely drafting or checking.
- The current destination state was re-read before mutation.
- A preview or diff contains only the intended post and metadata changes.
- The published artifact can be read back and, when applicable, its public URL
  can be checked.
- The source draft remains available until publication is verified.

Report failed checks, unverified checks, and remaining risk separately.
